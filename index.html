Quiet Cycle

Privacy policy

App “Quiet Cycle” (com.quietcycle.app). Version of November 1, 2026

In short: your entries stay only on your device, encrypted. The app has no internet access, uses no analytics and sends nothing anywhere.

What data is processed

Only the entries you make yourself: period days, symptoms, mood, temperature, weight, sleep, test results, date of birth of your baby, notes and settings. They are stored on the device and used only for calculations within the App. The Developer has no access to them.

What the App does not do

• it does not connect to the internet (on Android the App has no network permission); • it uses no analytics, ads, trackers, push services or third-party SDKs that send data; • it requires no sign-up, email or phone number; • it is not included in Google or iCloud cloud backups; • it does not sell or share data with anyone.

Protection

Entries are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is kept in the system’s secure storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain), and when a PIN is set it is derived from the PIN (PBKDF2). If you forget your PIN, data can only be restored from your own backup. Appearance settings (theme, color, icon) are kept separately from your entries so that the unlock screen looks as usual. Biometrics (optional). If you turn on fingerprint or Face ID unlock, your fingerprint and face are checked by the system itself — the App only receives a “yes” or “no”. For this, the data key is additionally kept in the system’s secure storage.

Permissions

• Notifications and vibration — for reminders you turned on yourself. They are created on the device, without servers. • Run after restart (Android) — so reminders survive a phone restart. • Biometrics — only if you turned on fingerprint or Face ID unlock. • Purchases (Google Play / App Store) — only if you buy “Quiet Cycle Plus” or support the developer. Payment is handled by the store app; the App only receives a purchase confirmation from it, without card details, and the store receives none of your entries from the App. The App contacts the store only while the “Quiet Cycle Plus” screen is open, when you buy or restore purchases, and on the next launch if a payment did not finish. The purchase status is kept on the device.

Widget and icon

Widget (Android, “Quiet Cycle Plus”, optional). If you add the widget to your home screen, the App stores the cycle start date and the expected period date for it on the device, unencrypted; the widget is visible on screen even when the diary is locked. When the widget is removed, this data is erased. A disguised icon (“Notes”, “Calculator”) and colored icons only change how the App looks on your phone.

Import, export and deletion

When importing, the App reads only the file you chose yourself and sends nothing anywhere. Backups, CSV spreadsheets and doctor summaries (text or PDF) are created only when you ask and saved where you choose. Backups are password-encrypted; spreadsheets and summaries are not. You can delete all data in settings (“Delete all data”) or by deleting the App.

If you write to the Developer

If you write to rizzlershrekizzler@gmail.com, the Developer receives your email address and message and uses them only to reply and resolve your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Ordinary questions are deleted no later than 6 months after the reply. Complaints and refund requests are kept for 2 years — the period of liability for purchased features working as described — or longer if needed to defend a claim. Please do not send diary entries or other health information unless your question requires it.

Purchases and store reports

Purchases are handled by Google Play and the App Store as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. The stores give the Developer only sales reports (order number, product, price, country and region of purchase, sometimes a postal code) — without your name, card details or diary entries. These reports are kept for accounting and tax purposes for as long as the law requires (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Your rights

The Developer is the controller of data from emails and sales reports (contact below). You can request access to this data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing, and object to processing — write to rizzlershrekizzler@gmail.com. If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority in your country.
